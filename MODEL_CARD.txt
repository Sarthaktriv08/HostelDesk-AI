Important limitations:
1. The uploaded workbook's Read Me explicitly says its 10,000 survey rows are synthetic/illustrative, not real CU student responses.
2. It does not contain one labeled complaint text per survey row. The complaint classifier is trained on a separate synthetic text dataset whose categories are informed by the survey.
3. The held-out test split groups paraphrases by template, but all examples are still synthetic. This score is not verified real-world accuracy.
4. Model confidence is not a guarantee. Safety-critical complaints should be escalated using explicit rules and human review.
